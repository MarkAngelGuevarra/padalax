# 📖 PadalaX Complete User Guide & Documentation

Welcome to **PadalaX** — the low-cost, decentralized cross-border remittance protocol built on **Stellar and Soroban** for Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) and their families.

---

## 📱 Part 1: How to Send Money (For OFWs Abroad)

### Step 1: Connect Your Stellar Wallet
1. Open [padalax.vercel.app](https://padalax.vercel.app) on your browser or mobile phone.
2. Click **"Connect Wallet"** in the top navigation bar.
3. Choose your preferred wallet provider:
   * **Freighter** (Recommended for Chrome / Brave / Edge)
   * **Albedo** (Recommended for Mobile web)
   * **xBull**, **LOBSTR**, or **Rabet**

### Step 2: Choose Single or Batch Mode
* **Single Remittance:** Send allowance or funds to a single family member.
* **Batch Remittance Mode (Max 5):** Click *"Switch to Multi-Recipient Batch Mode"* to generate up to 5 independent family vouchers (e.g., Mom Groceries, Son Tuition, Sister Medicine) in a single transaction.

### Step 3: Enter Remittance Details & Generate PIN
1. Select asset (XLM or USDC) and enter the remittance amount.
2. Review the live Philippine Peso conversion on the **OFW FX Ticker**.
3. Set your secret 6-digit Claim PIN (or click *"Generate Random PIN"*).
4. Click **"Lock & Generate Remittance Voucher"** and approve the transaction in your wallet.

### Step 4: Share Voucher with Your Family
1. Click the **"Share via WhatsApp"** or **"Share via Telegram"** button to automatically send the claim link and secret PIN to your relative.
2. Click **"Print Official Receipt"** to save or print a PDF proof of remittance.

---

## 🇵🇭 Part 2: How to Claim Funds (For Family Members in the Philippines)

### Step 1: Open the Claim Voucher Tab
1. Open the claim link sent by your family member on your smartphone.
2. Enter the **Remittance ID** and the secret **6-Digit Claim PIN**.

### Step 2: Choose Your Payout Method
Select how you would like to receive your money:
* **GCash Mobile Wallet**
* **Maya Wallet**
* **BDO / BPI / UnionBank Account**
* **QRPh Retail Pickup** (Palawan Express, Cebuana Lhuillier)

### Step 3: Zero-Gas Instant Settlement
1. Click **"Claim Voucher"**.
2. PadalaX's **Gasless FeeBump Relayer** pays 100% of the network transaction fees.
3. Funds settle in under 5 seconds with zero deduction.

---

## 🔒 Part 3: Sender Refund Protection (If Unclaimed)

If the recipient does not claim the remittance before the expiration window (default: 7 days):
1. Navigate to the **"Refund"** tab.
2. Enter your Remittance ID.
3. Click **"Execute Autonomous Refund"**.
4. 100% of the escrowed funds are returned directly to your wallet.
